Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CalorieCart ("the app", "we") is operated by Andrew McPhee, Australia. Contact: hermes@mcpheemgmt.com.au. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and sign-in identifier (email/password or Google sign-in).
  • Nutrition data you enter: your goals, diary entries, and search targets (for example calories or protein).
  • Location: your approximate or precise location, only while you use the app, to find nearby meals. You can deny this in iOS Settings; you can still search by choosing a place manually.
  • Photos: only when you choose to scan a meal. The photo is sent for analysis and is not used for advertising.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription. We never see your card details; Apple processes payment.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic usage data to fix bugs.

How we use it

To run the app: find meals near you, keep your diary, analyse meal photos, manage your free search limit and subscription, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.

Who processes it for us

We use these third-party services. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, crash reports and basic app analytics): your account details, diary and device/usage diagnostics.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it: your name and email.
  • Google Maps / Places: your location or chosen area, to find nearby venues and convert it to an address.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: your subscription status and anonymous purchase identifier.
  • OpenAI: meal photos and text you submit for analysis.
  • FatSecret: food search terms, to look up nutrition data.
  • Menu and image providers (including Uber Eats listings and SearchAPI image search): venue and meal names. They do not receive your name or email.

These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States. None of them are given your data to sell or for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and data, email hermes@mcpheemgmt.com.au from your registered address and we will delete it within 30 days. Some records (such as purchase receipts) are held by Apple under its own policies.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your information, or make a privacy complaint, using the email above. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

CalorieCart is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Health disclaimer

CalorieCart is for general information only and is not medical or dietary advice. Nutrition values for restaurant meals are estimates and may differ from what you are served.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
